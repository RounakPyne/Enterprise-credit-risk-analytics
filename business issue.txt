Business Problem

Enterprise Credit Risk & Portfolio Monitoring System

A financial institution has observed:

Rising customer defaults
Increasing delinquency rates
Growing fraud incidents
Higher collection costs
Uncertainty regarding future portfolio risk

Management wants answers to five key questions:

1. Which customers are most likely to default?

The company currently reacts after customers miss payments.

They want to proactively identify risky customers before default occurs.

2. What factors drive default risk?

Questions such as:

Does high credit utilization increase risk?
Does payment behavior matter?
Does fraud history influence default?
3. Which customer segments are riskiest?

Examples:

Young customers vs older customers
Salaried vs self-employed
High-income vs low-income
4. How effective is collections recovery?

Questions:

Are collection efforts working?
Which delinquency buckets recover best?
Where is money being lost?
5. What will happen in the future?

Management wants:

Default forecasts
Delinquency forecasts
Recovery forecasts

so they can allocate resources properly.
